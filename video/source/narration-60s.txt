Meet Easy. File conversion, right where you work.

Hold Option, drag a file, and drop it onto a format in the ring. That's it.

Convert images, documents, spreadsheets, and PDFs, with support for archives, data, and more. Resize images. Merge PDFs. Reuse your content.

Easy adds ONLYOFFICE integration for Office-to-PDF conversion and rebuilding PDFs into editable Word documents. Install Desktop Editors first; reconstructed layouts can reflow.

Add FFmpeg for video and audio conversion, animated GIFs, and frame extraction.

Save your favorite settings as presets. Process files in batches, automate watch folders, or use the command line.

Conversion happens on your Mac. Once the required engines are installed, it works offline, with originals protected from overwrite.

Built on the open-source Filee project. Easy. Drag. Drop. Converted.
